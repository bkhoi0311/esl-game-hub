CAMERA DUNG CHUNG CHO MAN TUONG TAC CLASSIN (danh cho IT, lam 1 lan moi may)

Muc dich: lop ClassIn va cac game camera (mo bang file .edu) cung dung camera S1 mot luc.
Windows chi cho 1 phan mem giu camera. Bo cai nay dung OBS Studio (mien phi, ma nguon mo)
doc S1 mot lan roi phat ra camera ao "OBS Virtual Camera" cho nhieu phan mem cung dung.

CACH CAI
1. Tai 2 file Cai-dat-camera-dung-chung.bat va setup.ps1 vao cung 1 thu muc tren may cua man tuong tac.
2. Bam dup Cai-dat-camera-dung-chung.bat, chon Yes khi Windows hoi quyen Administrator.
3. Doi 1-3 phut (tai va cai OBS). Cua so bao XONG la duoc.
4. Mo ClassIn > Cai dat > Camera > chon "OBS Virtual Camera" (1 lan).
5. Kiem tra: trong lop ClassIn, mo file Kiem-tra-camera.edu, bam "Bat dau kiem tra".

SAU KHI CAI
- OBS tu chay an moi khi bat may (bieu tuong o khay he thong goc phai). Giao vien khong can lam gi.
- Game camera thay S1 dang ban se tu dung "OBS Virtual Camera".

GO BO
- Xoa loi tat "Camera dung chung ClassIn" trong thu muc:
  C:\ProgramData\Microsoft\Windows\Start Menu\Programs\StartUp
- Go OBS Studio trong Settings > Apps (neu muon).
- Trong ClassIn chon lai camera S1.

LUU Y
- Can Internet luc cai (tai OBS tu github.com/obsproject/obs-studio). Bo cai kiem tra chu ky so truoc khi chay.
- Khong ghi hinh, khong gui hinh di dau.
- Windows 11 ban moi (build 26100.3321 tro len) khong can OBS: bo cai se mo thang Settings > Cameras de
  bat "Allow multiple apps to use camera at the same time".
